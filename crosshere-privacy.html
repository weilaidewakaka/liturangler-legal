来此合影 隐私政策

本政策说明来此合影（CrossHere，以下简称"本应用"）收集哪些信息、如何使用、与谁共享，以及您如何删除它们。

我们收集的信息

  • 账号信息：通过"通过 Apple 登录"获得的账号标识；首次授权时 Apple 提供的姓名（用作默认昵称）。我们不收集手机号、邮箱、生日等其他资料
  • 个人资料：您设置的昵称与头像
  • 记录：您拍摄或上传的照片（以及我们生成的缩略图）、您写的文字、记录的坐标与拍摄时间、您为记录设置的可见范围
  • 好友关系：您的邀请码、好友与待确认的好友请求、您给好友打的标签组（包括拉黑名单）
  • 订阅状态：当前订阅档位与到期时间。付款由 Apple 处理，我们拿不到您的支付信息

位置与照片

  • 只有在您点"现场拍照"创建记录时，本应用才会请求并读取定位，用来确定这条记录的坐标。我们不使用后台定位，不持续追踪您的位置
  • 上传已有照片时，本应用只读取您选中的那一张照片的 GPS 位置，不读取照片的其他元数据，也不访问相册中的其他照片
  • 如果您之前授权过定位，地图上会显示您与好友记录之间的距离，这个计算只在您的设备上进行，不会上传您的实时位置

谁能看到您的信息

  • 您的记录坐标会对您选择的好友可见。每条记录默认仅您自己可见；您把可见范围设为"全部好友"或某些标签组后，这些好友能在地图上看到记录的位置、创建时间和您的昵称头像
  • 好友只有在自己也在附近留下过记录时，才能看到照片和文字
  • 您的昵称和头像对您的好友与向您发送好友请求的人可见
  • 您给好友打的标签（包括拉黑名单）只有您自己能看到，对方不会知道
  • 我们不出售您的信息，不投放广告，不使用任何第三方追踪或分析工具

存储与服务商

您的数据存储在我们使用的云服务 Supabase 上（数据中心位于日本东京，AWS ap-northeast-1）。照片与文字只通过短时有效的临时链接下发，好友设备上不会缓存它们的照片或文字。

删除

  • 您可以随时删除任意一条记录，照片和文字会从服务器上永久删除
  • 您可以在应用的主页中注销账号，您的账号、全部记录、照片、头像与好友关系会被永久删除，无法恢复
  • 登出或注销后，本机上的草稿与登录信息会被清空

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策更新

政策有更新时，我们会修改本页面上的更新日期。

CrossHere Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information CrossHere ("the App") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Information We Collect

  • Account: the account identifier from Sign in with Apple, and the name Apple provides the first time you sign in (used as your default nickname). We do not collect your phone number, email, birthday, or other details
  • Profile: the nickname and avatar you set
  • Records: the photos you take or upload (and the thumbnails we generate), the text you write, each record's location and capture time, and the visibility you choose for it
  • Friends: your invite code, your friends and pending friend requests, and the tag groups you assign to friends (including your block list)
  • Subscription: your current subscription tier and expiration date. Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information

Location and Photos

  • The App asks for and reads your location only when you tap "Take a photo here" to create a record, to set that record's location. We do not use background location and do not track you continuously
  • When you upload an existing photo, the App reads only the GPS location of the one photo you select. It does not read the photo's other metadata or access any other photos in your library
  • If you have already granted location access, the map can show how far you are from a friend's record. This is calculated on your device; your live location is never uploaded

Who Can See Your Information

  • Your record locations are visible to the friends you choose. Every record is visible only to you by default. When you set a record to "All friends" or to certain tag groups, those friends can see its location, creation time, and your nickname and avatar on the map
  • Friends can see the photo and text only if they have also left a record nearby
  • Your nickname and avatar are visible to your friends and to people who send you friend requests
  • The tags you give friends (including your block list) are visible only to you; they are never told
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use any third-party tracking or analytics tools

Storage and Service Providers

Your data is stored with our cloud provider, Supabase, in a data center in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1). Photos and text are delivered only through short-lived links, and friends' devices never cache them.

Deletion

  • You can delete any record at any time; its photo and text are permanently deleted from our servers
  • You can delete your account from the Profile tab. Your account, all records, photos, avatar, and friendships are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered
  • Signing out or deleting your account clears drafts and sign-in data from your device

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

When this policy changes, we will update the date on this page.

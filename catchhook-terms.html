Service Description

CatchHook is a fishing journal and sharing app. You record the catches from each outing on iPhone (photos, species, counts, and more), and the App uses the location in your photos and weather data to turn them into stats, badges, and share cards. Apple Watch is an optional way to collect data, recording your GPS route, heart rate, step count, and other workout data.

鱼乐日记 隐私政策

鱼乐日记（以下简称"本应用"或"我们"）深知个人信息对您的重要性。我们致力于按照适用法律法规（包括但不限于《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》《中华人民共和国数据安全法》以及《欧盟通用数据保护条例 GDPR》）的要求，采取适当的技术与管理措施，保障您的个人信息安全。

本政策旨在明确说明我们在提供鱼乐日记服务过程中如何处理您的信息，包括信息的收集、使用、共享、存储及您的权利等内容。

我们处理的信息类型及处理目的

鱼乐日记以本地优先为设计原则，没有账号体系，我们不会主动收集或保存任何可识别您个人身份的信息，也没有自己的服务器。在您使用本应用过程中，可能涉及以下信息类型及处理方式：

运动与健康数据

在您使用 Apple Watch 记录时，本应用通过 Apple HealthKit 读写与本次记录相关的健康数据，包括运动时长、心率、步数等，仅用于生成钓鱼记录。所有数据完整保存在您设备的健康数据库中，不会传输至任何外部服务器；开启 iCloud 备份时，心率、步数、活动消耗等健康数据也不会备份到 iCloud。您可随时在 iPhone「健康」App 中管理或删除相关数据。

位置信息

本应用在以下场景使用位置信息：

  • 使用 Apple Watch 记录时，可选择性地记录 GPS 路径，用于生成轨迹和距离统计
  • 您添加的鱼获照片如包含拍摄位置，本应用会读取该位置，用于在地图上展示渔获并获取当地天气
  • 您保存的标点会记录您选择的位置

位置数据保存在设备本地；开启 iCloud 备份时，随记录一起备份到您自己的 iCloud（见下文「iCloud 备份」），不会上传至我们的服务器。您主动分享记录图片时，图片可能带有卫星地图底图，从而显示记录的大致位置，请在分享前留意。您可随时在系统设置中关闭位置权限。

天气信息

本应用通过 Apple WeatherKit 获取记录地点当时的天气（温度、风速、天气状况等），以及天气页面中的未来七天天气。获取天气时，相应的位置坐标会发送给 Apple 的 WeatherKit 服务处理。天气数据随记录保存，获取失败不影响记录的正常保存。

照片

您可以为每条鱼获添加照片。本应用会读取照片中的拍摄时间与位置（如有），用于记录鱼获时间、在地图上展示渔获和获取天气。用于创建标点的照片只读取位置，不会被保存。鱼获照片保存在设备本地，超过 2 MB 的会被压缩；开启 iCloud 备份时，照片原文件（含其中的拍摄信息）随记录一起备份到您自己的 iCloud，不会上传至我们的服务器。

购买信息

本应用通过 Apple App Store 的自动续期订阅（普通版与 Apple Watch 版）提供服务。付款由 Apple 全程处理，我们不会收集或存储您的支付卡号或账单信息。本应用仅通过 Apple StoreKit 验证购买凭证以解锁相应功能。

数据存储与安全

您的数据存储在 iPhone 或 Apple Watch 本地，不会传输至我们的服务器。本应用依赖 Apple 平台提供的安全机制（包括设备加密和应用沙盒）保护您的数据。用于标识本地用户的匿名标识符存储在设备 Keychain 中，受系统级安全保护，不会备份到 iCloud。

iCloud 备份

本应用可将您的数据备份到您自己的 iCloud，用于换机或在另一台 iPhone 上找回数据。您可在本应用「设置 → 基础设置 → iCloud 备份」随时开启或关闭。

  • 备份内容：已保存的钓鱼记录（含鱼获照片与 Apple Watch 路径）、标点、徽章封面。心率、步数、活动消耗等健康数据不会备份；编辑后未保存的内容不会备份；订阅状态与本地用户标识不会备份。
  • 存放位置：数据存放在您 Apple 账户的 iCloud 私有空间中，受 Apple 加密保护，我们无法访问，也不经过我们的服务器。
  • 多台设备：一台设备上新保存的记录会出现在登录同一 iCloud 的其他设备上；对已有记录的修改与删除不会同步到其他设备。
  • 账号变化：iCloud 账号变更或云端数据被清除时，备份会自动关闭，本机数据不受影响；您再次开启时会先征得您的确认。
  • 卸载与清除：卸载本应用不会删除 iCloud 中的数据。如需清除，请前往 iPhone 系统「设置」App →「Apple 账户 → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间 → 鱼乐日记 → 删除数据」。
  • iCloud 中的备份依赖您的 iCloud 钥匙串解密。如您丢失了所有受信任的设备且无法恢复 Apple 账户，iCloud 中的备份可能无法恢复。

数据共享

我们不会将您的任何个人信息出售或出租，以下情况除外：

  • Apple 平台服务（HealthKit、MapKit、WeatherKit、StoreKit、iCloud）根据 Apple 的隐私政策处理相关数据
  • 法律法规要求强制披露的情形

您的权利

由于本应用采用本地优先设计，您的数据完全由您掌控：

  • 访问与导出：您可在应用内导出钓鱼记录数据
  • 删除：您可在应用内删除任意钓鱼记录（开启 iCloud 备份时，iCloud 中的对应备份也会删除），或卸载应用以清除本地数据；iCloud 中的备份可按上文「iCloud 备份」所述在系统设置中清除
  • 健康数据管理：您可在 iPhone「健康」App 中管理通过 HealthKit 写入的数据
  • 权限撤回：您可随时在系统设置中撤回位置、健康等权限

未成年人保护

本应用不面向14岁以下未成年人。如您是未成年人，请在监护人陪同下使用本应用。

政策更新

我们可能不定期更新本隐私政策。政策变更时，我们将通过应用内提示告知您。继续使用本应用即表示您接受更新后的政策内容。

联系我们

如您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，欢迎联系我们：support@liturangler.com

CatchHook Privacy Policy

CatchHook ("the App" or "we") takes your privacy seriously. We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, including the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China (PIPL), the Data Security Law of the PRC, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This policy explains how we handle information in connection with the CatchHook service, including what is collected, how it is used, how it is shared, how it is stored, and your rights.

Types of Information We Process

CatchHook is a local-first app with no account system and no servers of its own. We do not actively collect or retain any information that personally identifies you. The following types of information may be involved when you use the app:

Motion & Health Data

When you record with Apple Watch, the app reads and writes health data for that record via Apple HealthKit, including workout duration, heart rate, and step count, solely for generating your fishing records. All data is stored in your device's Health database and is never transmitted to any external server; with iCloud Backup on, health data such as heart rate, steps, and active calories is not backed up to iCloud either. You can manage or delete this data at any time in the iPhone Health app.

Location Data

The app uses location data in the following cases:

  • When recording with Apple Watch, it optionally records a GPS route to generate your trail and distance statistics
  • If a catch photo you add carries a capture location, the app reads it to show the catch on the map and to fetch local weather
  • Spots you save store the location you choose

Location data is stored locally on your device. With iCloud Backup on, it is backed up with your records to your own iCloud (see "iCloud Backup" below). It is never uploaded to our servers. When you choose to share a record image, it may include a satellite map background that reveals the record's approximate location, so please keep this in mind before sharing. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings.

Weather Data

The app uses Apple WeatherKit to fetch the weather at a record's location at the time (temperature, wind speed, conditions), as well as the seven-day forecast on the Weather page. When weather is fetched, the relevant coordinates are sent to Apple's WeatherKit service for processing. Weather data is stored with your record, and a failure to fetch weather does not affect saving your record.

Photos

You can add a photo to each catch. The app reads the photo's capture time and location (if present) to record when the catch was made, show it on the map, and fetch weather. A photo used to create a spot is read for its location only and is not saved. Catch photos are stored locally on your device; photos larger than 2 MB are compressed. With iCloud Backup on, the photo files (including the capture information they contain) are backed up with your records to your own iCloud. They are never uploaded to our servers.

Purchase Information

The app is offered through auto-renewable subscriptions (Standard Plan and Apple Watch Plan) on the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store your payment card number or billing information. The app uses Apple StoreKit only to verify purchase entitlements and unlock features.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored locally on your iPhone or Apple Watch and is never transmitted to our servers. The app relies on Apple's platform security mechanisms — including device encryption and app sandboxing — to protect your data. A local anonymous user identifier is stored in the device Keychain, protected by system-level security, and is not backed up to iCloud.

iCloud Backup

The app can back up your data to your own iCloud, so you can get it back on a new iPhone or on another iPhone. You can turn it on or off at any time under Settings → General → iCloud Backup in the app.

  • What is backed up: saved fishing trips (with catch photos and Apple Watch routes), spots, and badge covers. Health data such as heart rate, steps, and active calories is not backed up; unsaved edits are not backed up; your subscription status and local user identifier are not backed up.
  • Where it is kept: in the private iCloud storage of your Apple Account, protected by Apple's encryption. We cannot access it, and it never passes through our servers.
  • More than one device: a trip newly saved on one device appears on your other devices signed in to the same iCloud; changes to or deletions of existing trips are not synced to other devices.
  • Account changes: if your iCloud account changes or your iCloud data is deleted, backup turns off automatically and the data on this device is not affected. When you turn it back on, the app asks you to confirm first.
  • Uninstalling and deleting: uninstalling the app does not delete your data in iCloud. To delete it, open the iPhone Settings app and go to Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → CatchHook → Delete Data.
  • Your iCloud backup is decrypted with your iCloud Keychain. If you lose all your trusted devices and cannot recover your Apple Account, the backup in iCloud may not be recoverable.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Exceptions:

  • Apple platform services (HealthKit, MapKit, WeatherKit, StoreKit, iCloud) process relevant data under Apple's privacy policy
  • When required by applicable law

Your Rights

Because CatchHook is local-first, you are in full control of your data:

  • Access & Export: You can export your fishing trips from within the app
  • Deletion: You can delete any fishing trip in the app (with iCloud Backup on, its backup in iCloud is deleted too), or uninstall the app to clear all local data; your backup in iCloud can be deleted in Settings as described under "iCloud Backup" above
  • Health Data: You can manage HealthKit-written data in the iPhone Health app
  • Revoking Permissions: You can revoke location and health permissions at any time in your device settings

Minors

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or 14 where applicable law requires). If you are a minor, please use the app under parental supervision.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will notify you through an in-app prompt. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
